## Project Overview

Paws & Whiskers Rescue is a non-profit animal rescue organisation based in Cape Town, South Africa. This project involves the design and development of a professional and responsive website for the organisation.

The website aims to:
- Increase awareness of the organisation's mission and work
- Facilitate the adoption process
- Recruit volunteers and attract sponsors
- Provide educational resources about animal welfare

---

## Website Goals and Objectives
-Increase Awareness: Reach a wider audience and communicate the organisation's mission 
-Facilitate Adoption: Provide a clear path for potential adopters to find and enquire about animals 
-Recruit Volunteers:  Attract passionate individuals to support the organisation 
-Attract Sponsors:  Secure sponsorships to fund operations 

### Key Performance Indicators
- 40% increase in website traffic within six months
- 50+ adoption enquiries per month
- 30% growth in volunteer registrations in the first quarter
- 5+ new sponsors within the first year

---

## Key Features and Functionality
Homepage (index.html); Hero image, mission statement, call-to-action buttons, navigation menu 
About Us (about.html); Organisation history, mission and vision, team members, core values 
Adoption (services.html); Available animals, adoption process, success stories 
Get Involved (enquiry.html); Volunteer registration form, sponsor enquiry form
Contact (contact.html); Multiple location addresses, contact form, social media links
