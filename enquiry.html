Get Involved With Paws & Whiskers Rescue

There are many ways you can make a difference in the lives of animals in need. Whether you want to volunteer your time, become a sponsor, or make a donation, every contribution helps (Animal Welfare South Africa, 2022).

Volunteer With Us

We are always looking for passionate volunteers to help with:

  • Animal care and socialisation
  • Administrative support
  • Event coordination and fundraising
  • Transport and logistics
  • Foster care for animals awaiting adoption

Fill out the form below to register your interest in volunteering.

Become a Sponsor

Corporate sponsors play a vital role in our ability to rescue and rehabilitate animals. Sponsorship opportunities include:

  • Platinum Sponsor (ZAR 50,000+) — Major branding and naming rights
  • Gold Sponsor (ZAR 25,000 - 49,999) — Featured on website and events
  • Silver Sponsor (ZAR 10,000 - 24,999) — Logo on website and social media
  • Bronze Sponsor (ZAR 5,000 - 9,999) — Acknowledgment on website

Complete the enquiry form below and a member of our team will contact you to discuss sponsorship opportunities.
